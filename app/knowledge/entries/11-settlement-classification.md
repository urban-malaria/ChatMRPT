summary: >
  Settlement classification is a manual workflow: selected wards are divided into grid
  cells (500 m by default) over satellite imagery, and the user labels each cell as
  Formal, Informal, Slum, Rural or No Buildings/Avoid Area. Results can be exported as
  maps and ward summaries.
status: draft
sources: []
---
- Wards can be chosen by name, drawn on the map, or taken from the top-ranked wards after risk analysis.
- Labelling is done by the user; ChatMRPT does not classify settlements automatically.
- Exports include the labelled grid, a ward summary and a map image.
