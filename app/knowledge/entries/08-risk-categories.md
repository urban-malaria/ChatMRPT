summary: >
  After ranking, wards are split into three equal-sized groups: the top third High Risk,
  the middle third Medium Risk, the bottom third Low Risk. Categories are relative to the
  other wards in the upload, not fixed thresholds. With several years of data, each
  year's rank averages the environmental composite rank and the malaria burden rank.
status: draft

---
- Categories come from rank position, so every analysis has roughly a third of wards in each group, however high or low risk is overall.
- The "All Years" vulnerability map uses environmental variables only.
- Per-year maps combine environmental risk with that year's burden: combined rank = (composite rank + burden rank) / 2.
- Rankings support prioritisation decisions; they do not show what causes malaria in a ward.
