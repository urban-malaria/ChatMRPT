**If you have no data yet:** click **Try Sample Data**. It is on the welcome screen, in the upload window ("Use sample data instead") and in the sidebar under Sample data. It loads a Kwara State DHIS2 export of monthly health-facility malaria testing (6,948 records) into the current chat.

**Then:** type **start the TPR workflow**, answer **yes**, and choose the facility level and age group. ChatMRPT calculates malaria burden for each ward, then you can map it, rank wards by risk ("run malaria risk analysis") and plan bed nets.

**With your own data:** click the upload button (paper clip) and choose your DHIS2 export (CSV or Excel). The same steps follow.
