- **Admin levels:** Country > State > LGA > Ward > Health facility.
- **Facility levels:** primary (PHCs, clinics), secondary (general hospitals), tertiary (specialist and teaching hospitals).
- **RDT / microscopy:** the two malaria test methods recorded in facility data.
- **ITN:** insecticide-treated net. **IRS:** indoor residual spraying. **SMC:** seasonal malaria chemoprevention.
- **DHIS2:** the national health information system; exports often use orgunitlevel2/3/4 for State/LGA/Ward.
