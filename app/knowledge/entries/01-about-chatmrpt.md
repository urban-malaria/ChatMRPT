**What it does**
- Calculates ward-level malaria burden (positive cases per 1,000 population) from health facility testing data.
- Adds environmental and settlement variables for each ward and ranks wards by risk using two methods: composite scoring and principal component analysis (PCA).
- Classifies wards as High, Medium or Low Risk and produces maps and downloadable tables.
- Allocates a fixed stock of insecticide-treated nets (ITNs) to wards in order of risk.
- Supports manual settlement classification of high-risk wards on a grid.
- Answers questions about the uploaded data (summaries, rankings, charts) and general malaria questions.

**What it does not do**
- It does not forecast or predict future malaria cases.
- It does not give clinical or medical advice.
- Its risk rankings are for prioritisation; they are not causal models of what drives malaria.
- It does not collect data; it works with what the user uploads plus the geospatial layers built into the tool.

**How to use it**
Upload files with the upload button, or try the built-in sample data from the sidebar. Then follow the guided workflow or ask questions in plain language.
